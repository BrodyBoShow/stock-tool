Questions

Is this investment advice?+

No. StockBud is an informational and educational research tool — it gives impersonal analysis, never personalized recommendations to buy or sell. It is not an investment adviser or broker, and nothing in it is financial advice. You make your own decisions.

Where does the data come from?+

Fundamentals and filings come from SEC EDGAR, macro data from the Federal Reserve (FRED), and prices from a licensed market-data provider. Prices are end-of-day / delayed — your brokerage statement is always the source of truth for your holdings.

Do you connect to my brokerage?+

Optionally, and strictly read-only via a trusted connector (SnapTrade). StockBud can see positions and history to power your analytics — it can never place trades or move money.
